# WilmAI Home 0.1.0 Beta

The first beta of WilmAI Home, a desktop app for students and parents:
messages, timetables, homework, exams and grades in one place, a notification
when something new needs you, and optional AI that picks out what matters.

- For Apple Silicon Macs (macOS 13.3 or later) and Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
  On Windows, notifications and local AI aren't there yet.
- Neither download is signed yet. On a Mac, macOS stops the app the first time:
  choose Done, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. On
  Windows, choose More info, then Run anyway.
- AI is optional and starts off. In Settings, choose OpenAI with your own API key
  (usually tens of cents a month, with a monthly limit you set, 50 cents to $3),
  or local AI on a Mac (a 9.1 GB download; needs 16 GB of memory, and it's
  slower and less accurate). How to get a key: https://wilm.ai/en/home#openai-key
- When there's a new version, the app tells you; download it from
  https://wilm.ai/app.

It's a beta, built by one parent. AI can misread things, dates especially, so
check anything important in the message itself. Problems and ideas:
https://github.com/aikarjal/wilmai/issues
