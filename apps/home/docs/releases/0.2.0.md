# WilmAI Home 0.2.0 Beta

Children at schools on different Wilmas, together in one app. · Eri koulujen Wilmoissa olevat lapset samassa sovelluksessa.

- Several Wilmas in one app: when your children's schools are on different
  Wilmas (another town, an upper secondary school, a private school), sign in
  to each one, and Today, the inbox, the week and the notifications show all
  the children together. Add one with "Add another school's Wilma" on the
  sign-in page. Thanks for asking for this in issue #21.
- When Wilma lists a child twice (a new school gives a new profile), untick the
  one you don't need. What was read from it stays.
- One sign-in page for everything: who uses the app, each school's Wilma with
  its children, and one Sign in. After signing out, your username and kept
  password are filled in, so signing in again is one click. "Wilma accounts"
  in the menu, under Settings, opens the page.
- The sign-in page has its own settings: the app's language, the theme,
  whether the app stays signed in, and deleting a family's data or resetting
  the app.
- A theme choice: automatic, light or dark, also at the foot of the menu.
- Settings is shorter: translating messages is now part of the WilmAI
  Intelligence card, the OpenAI key, limit and usage sit behind one section,
  and opening new messages in the background shows only when an AI is on.

Problems and ideas: https://github.com/aikarjal/wilmai/issues or feedback@wilm.ai
