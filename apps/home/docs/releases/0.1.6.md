# WilmAI Home 0.1.6 Beta

Make-up exams leave Today once they're past, and Today's items can be marked done. · Rästikokeet poistuvat Tänään-näkymästä, kun ne ovat ohi, ja Tänään-näkymän asiat voi merkitä hoidetuiksi.

- A make-up exam set for "the next lesson" no longer stays on Today with
  "Date not confirmed" when the app can't tell which lesson that was. It
  goes once a later lesson of the subject is over, or two weeks after the
  teacher's note.
- Make-up exams and Needs you on Today have the lists' Done button, with
  Undo.
- In the lists, a row's last line no longer runs under the Done button.
- A translated message's note says it was translated automatically, without
  naming the AI model.

Problems and ideas: https://github.com/aikarjal/wilmai/issues or feedback@wilm.ai
