# WilmAI Home 0.1.5 Beta

Messages translated into your language, and the app in Swedish. · Viestit käännettyinä omalle kielellesi, ja sovellus ruotsiksi.

- With OpenAI, messages, bulletins and notes in another language are
  translated as they come in, titles too: choose a language in Settings →
  Translating messages (Finnish, Swedish, English, Ukrainian or Arabic). The
  original is one click away. It usually costs a fraction of a cent a message
  and stays within your monthly limit. Thanks for the idea in issue #20.
- The app itself is now in Swedish too: Settings → App language.
- Messages you send show in the inbox straight away, with "no reply yet",
  instead of only once someone answers.
- The monthly AI limit also counts what's still being read, so the app no
  longer goes a few cents past it.

Problems and ideas: https://github.com/aikarjal/wilmai/issues or feedback@wilm.ai
