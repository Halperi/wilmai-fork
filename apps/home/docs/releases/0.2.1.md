# WilmAI Home 0.2.1 Beta

Inbox is now Messages, a changed school day needs you, and steadier sign-in. · Saapuneet on nyt Viestit, muuttunut koulupäivä odottaa sinua, ja kirjautuminen on varmempaa.

- Inbox is now Messages: bulletins and what you sent are there too, not only
  what came in. Its list of messages is Conversations. G M goes there (G I
  still works).
- A school day that runs differently (an earlier end, a later start, no
  lessons), when a message says so, is under Needs you in the week before the
  day, with the message's sentence about it. The day itself shows the change
  on Today, in the message's words. There's nothing to do, but a parent has to
  know when a young child's day ends early.
- Notes marks the absence to explain, the one the menu's badge counts, the way
  Today does.
- The AI reads a few things better: what to bring to an optional club or offer,
  and a standing rule like "always bring PE kit", aren't steps for you; a form
  or contract your child hands in (a TET contract) is; a teacher's note about
  an illness or an exam to make up reads as neutral, not a concern; and an
  info evening for your child's grade isn't optional because it needs no
  sign-up.
- Signing in is steadier: a sign-in Wilma answers late can't sign a Wilma back
  in after you signed out of it, or replace the sample family you picked
  meanwhile. The fix follows one Halperi wrote in their fork.

Problems and ideas: https://github.com/aikarjal/wilmai/issues or feedback@wilm.ai
